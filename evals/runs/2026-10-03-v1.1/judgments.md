## What the judges said again and again

- **Diagnosis is thin.** The baseline names competing causes, says what evidence would separate them, and uses domain-specific mechanisms such as fintech SDK startup costs, nursing transition-to-practice turnover, and email deliverability. The Skill gives diagnosis one table row.
- **Lens labels and the menu cost reading time.** Nine of eleven judges said this in some form. A few called specific lens tags weak fits.
- **Action order vs. table order.** When `Do this first` points to the Anchor while the table is "Ranked by Coverage," two judges found the order of action unclear. One run (gym) had Row 1 and the Anchor both claiming to come first.
- **The Skill was the more honest response.** The baseline was marked down for unsourced figures and frequency claims stated as fact. The Skill stated its assumptions openly.
