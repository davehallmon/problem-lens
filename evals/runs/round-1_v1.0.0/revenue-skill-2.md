Flat revenue is a symptom. The root is likely one of two things: your team has hit its billable-hour ceiling, or no one owns bringing in new work, so revenue only holds steady from existing clients (both are guesses, since you haven't confirmed capacity or where clients come from). The first move is to find out which one it is, because they need opposite fixes: one calls for pricing, the other for pipeline.

| 2 | If staff are near full, raise rates on new work or switch some services to fixed-fee packages. | First Principles | Inherited assumptions block progress. | Revenue equals hours times rate, so when hours are capped, rate is the only lever left without new hires. | Price-sensitive clients may leave, and this does nothing if the real gap is demand. |
