**Summary.** The leadership split is a symptom. The likely root is that no one knows why nurses leave, so both camps are guessing: one with a plan, one with trials. Assuming you have about 12 months of exit records that show tenure, shift, and manager, you can find where turnover clusters before you choose either path.

**Recommendation.**
- Do this first: Row 1 — break down the last year of exits by tenure, shift, and manager, because neither a plan nor a test can aim at a cause you have not found.
- Switch to Row 3 if the breakdown shows no clear cluster, since the cause is then too spread out to plan for and small tests will teach you faster.
