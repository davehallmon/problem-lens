**Summary.** Probably not yet: a full-time hire covers eight hours, but the stated problem lasts two. The likely root is that every booking, change, and question has to come through one phone line, so demand piles up from 8 to 10am. Assuming the practice has one receptionist who also checks in patients during the peak, and that waits are short the rest of the day.

**Recommendation.**
- Do this first: Anchor — clear all non-phone work off the front desk from 8 to 10am, because it costs nothing and shows whether the desk is short on time or short on people.
- Switch to Row 1 if most peak calls turn out to be routine bookings, changes, or confirmations.
