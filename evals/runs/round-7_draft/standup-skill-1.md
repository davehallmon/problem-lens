**Summary.** Async vs. live is a choice of fix; the likely root is that one 30-minute meeting for 14 people does two jobs at once, status broadcast and blocker solving, so most people sit through updates that don't touch their work. Assuming the standup runs 5 days a week, it costs 7 person-hours a day (14 × 30 minutes), about 35 a week. Assuming the 3 time zones leave some shared working hours, a short live slot can survive whatever you change.
