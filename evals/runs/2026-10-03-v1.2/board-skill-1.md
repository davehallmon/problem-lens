**Summary.** The new idea at each meeting is a symptom; the likely root is that the board has no shared facts about the 15% drop and no rule for how it picks a plan. Assuming you have an executive director but no fundraising staff, and the plan must be set before next fiscal year starts. Assuming no one on the board has been named to own the plan.

**What's Likely Going On**
- No decision rule or owner, so ideas pile up and none get closed — check: do past minutes show a vote, a named owner, or a deadline for any earlier idea? If none do, this is the cause.
- No shared facts about why giving fell, so each member fills the gap with a pet theory — check: can anyone say where the 15% went (fewer donors, smaller gifts, or one lost large gift or grant)? If no one can, this is the cause.
- Real conflict on goals or risk, such as grow vs. hold steady, events vs. grants (guess) — check: ask each member privately for a dollar target and how much board time they will give; a wide spread means the conflict is about goals, not ideas.

- Switch to Row 3 if the board already agrees on why giving fell and still can't pick a plan.
