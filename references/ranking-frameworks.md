# Ranking Frameworks

Default: Coverage.
Offer others on request.

## Coverage
Force diversity. Pick five solutions from five lens families. Merge overlaps.
Best when: the user needs options, not a single bet.

## Impact vs. Effort
Score each solution on impact and effort. Prefer high impact, low effort.
Best when: triaging a long list fast.

## Confidence vs. Reversibility
Score confidence (how sure) and reversibility (how easy to undo). Prefer high confidence, high reversibility.
Best when: stakes are high and information is low.

## ICE (Impact, Confidence, Ease)
Score 1–10 on each. Multiply. Rank.
Best when: comparing product or growth bets.

## RICE (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort)
Score reach times impact times confidence, divided by effort.
Best when: teams have usage data.

## Eisenhower (Urgent vs. Important)
Plot on two axes. Do, schedule, delegate, or drop.
Best when: too many tasks, not enough time.

## Regret Minimization
Ask which choice you will regret least in ten years.
Best when: the decision is hard to reverse and personal.

## Optionality
Score how many future doors each solution opens or closes.
Best when: the future is uncertain and platforms matter.

## Cost of Delay
Estimate the cost of waiting. Prefer solutions that cut delay fastest.
Best when: deadlines or queues drive the problem.
