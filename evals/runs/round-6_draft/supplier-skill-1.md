**Summary.** The urgent problem is a supply gap: about 1,200 orders of stock (300 a day for 4 days) and no proven way to refill it. The root problem is that one supplier made everything you sell, so one failure stops the business. Assuming the two backups have never seen your products, and your 4 days of stock sit in your own warehouse, the first job is to learn how fast anyone can refill you.

**What's Likely Going On**
- The backups can supply your top sellers within about a week — check: the lead time and minimum order each quotes for your top 10 SKUs.
- The bankrupt supplier still has goods made or in transit that can be bought or released — check: whether an administrator has been named and will sell finished stock or release orders you already paid for.
- Stock is uneven, so some best sellers run out in a day or two while others last weeks (a guess; 4 days is an average) — check: days of stock per SKU, top sellers first.

**Recommendation**
- Do this first: Row 1 — call both backup suppliers today with your top-SKU list, because their lead times decide whether you must slow sales.
- Switch to Row 3 if neither backup can ship your top sellers within 4 days.
