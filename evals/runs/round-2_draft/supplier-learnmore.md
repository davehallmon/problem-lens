**Cynefin Framework**
Dave Snowden's framework for matching your method to the type of problem: clear, complicated, complex, or chaotic.
It fit because losing your only supplier with 4 days of stock is a chaotic problem, so the first job is to stabilize it (Row 2) before you analyze it.
https://thecynefin.co/about-us/about-cynefin-framework/

**OODA Loop**
John Boyd's cycle of observe, orient, decide, act, built for fast-changing situations.
It fit because supplier answers, stock levels, and order volume will change hour by hour this week, faster than a fixed plan can keep up (Row 4).
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OODA_loop

**Coverage**
Pick one solution from each of five lens families, then order by expected effect on the root problem.
It fit because you need options across supply, demand, and process, not a single bet, and ordering by effect put getting new stock moving first.
