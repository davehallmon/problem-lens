This has one obvious fix, so a full options analysis won't help. Here is the direct answer.

**If the tray is empty:** load paper, then press OK or Resume on the printer.

**If the tray already has paper,** the printer can't feed it. Try these in order:

1. Take the stack out, fan it, square the edges, and put it back. Use 10 to 50 sheets, not a full tray.
2. Slide the paper guides so they touch the stack's edges without bending it.
3. Push the stack all the way in until it stops.
4. Check that the paper size and tray set in the print dialog match what's loaded.
5. Look inside for a torn scrap or jam. Turn the printer off before you reach in.
6. Wipe the feed rollers with a lint-free cloth dampened with water. Let them dry.
7. Turn the printer off, unplug it for 60 seconds, then plug it back in.

If it still says it's out of paper after all seven, the paper sensor or the feed rollers may be worn. Check the warranty or contact the maker's support.
