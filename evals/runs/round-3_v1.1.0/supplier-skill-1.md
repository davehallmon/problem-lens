**Summary**
The bankruptcy is the trigger, but the root is that one supplier held your whole range and no second source was ever tested. Assuming "4 days" is total stock at 300 orders a day, your best sellers likely run out sooner, and assuming the backups stock similar items, the real question is how long the gap will be. Today's job is to measure that gap; this week's job is to make sure one supplier can't stop you again.

**What's Likely Going On**
- Short gap: the backups hold your items in stock and can ship within days — check: ask each backup whether your top SKUs are in their warehouse or made to order, and get a firm ship date.
- Uneven runway: "4 days" is an average, and a few best sellers run out in one or two — check: days of stock per SKU for the items that make up most of your orders.
- Recoverable stock (guess): goods you already paid for, or that are in transit, may still ship from the bankrupt supplier — check: ask the administrator whether paid or in-transit orders will be released.
