**Summary**
The 60% drop is a symptom. The likely root is that step 3 asks for something the person who signed up can't give on the spot, such as admin access, company data, or a teammate. Assuming the 60% applies to users who reach step 3, that most of the 200 weekly signups get that far, and that you track events by step, the first job is to find out who drops and why before changing the step.

**What's Likely Going On**
- Step 3 needs something the signer doesn't have (access, data, a decision-maker). This is a guess. Check: what step 3 asks for, and whether people who drop come back within 7 days or forward the link to a colleague.
- Many signups aren't a fit and were only browsing, so step 3 is just the first place real effort is required. Check: whether drop-off rate differs a lot by signup source, company size, or work vs. personal email.
- Step 3 is broken, slow, or confusing on some setups. Check: error logs, page load time, and drop-off by browser and device for step 3 alone.

**Recommendation**
- Do this first: Row 1 — split step 3 drop-off by source, company size, and device, and watch 10 to 20 session recordings, because one afternoon of this can tell the three explanations apart.
