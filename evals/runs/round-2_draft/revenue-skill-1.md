**Summary.** Flat revenue is a symptom; the likely root is that no one owns winning new work, so growth rests on referrals and repeat clients that have stopped growing. Assuming the firm bills by the hour or project, has not raised rates lately, and staff are not fully booked, demand rather than capacity is the limit. Neither cause is confirmed yet, so the first move should tell you which one it is.

**Recommendation.**
- Do this first: Row 1 — build a revenue bridge for the last six quarters, because it shows in about a week which of rows 2–5 deserves your money.
- Switch to Row 4 if billable staff are already near full use, since then capacity, not demand, caps revenue.
