**Questions That Would Change the Pick**

2. Was the delivery date promised in writing, or did your terms call it an estimate? A written promise makes a chargeback more likely to succeed, which argues for a bigger offer in Row 3. Clear "estimate" terms give you more room.
3. Did the customer already ask for something specific on the phone? If so, start from that request, and Row 1 becomes a call to confirm it, not to ask.
