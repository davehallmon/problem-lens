**Summary.** The drop in teen visits is a symptom. The likely root is that the branch no longer fits what teens need after school, and no one has checked what that is. Assuming "use" means coming into the branch after school, that the pilot budget covers one program for about one school term, and that no one has yet compared this branch with other branches.

**What's Likely Going On**
- Something changed at the branch around 2022 (hours, rules, staff, a lost teen space) and pushed teens out — check: a list of branch changes since 2022 set against monthly teen visits; a sharp drop at one date points here.
- Teens' after-school habits moved elsewhere (a new rec center, a school program that runs later, phones and home) — guess — check: whether other branches lost teens at the same rate; a city-wide drop points here, a drop at this branch alone points to a local cause.
- The count changed, not the teens (a new way of logging age, or teens shifted to online use) — guess — check: whether the counting method changed in 2022, and whether teen e-book, Wi-Fi, or online use rose while visits fell.

**Recommendation**
- Do this first: Row 1 — match monthly teen visits against a list of changes since 2022 and against other branches, because it tells you whether to undo a local change or chase a wider shift before you spend the one pilot.
- Switch to Row 3 if the drop matches other branches or no single change lines up, because then the answer sits with teens and you need their input to pick the pilot.
