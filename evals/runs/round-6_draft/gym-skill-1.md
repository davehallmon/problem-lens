**Summary.** Three failed discounts point to a root problem: you don't yet know why these members left, and price may not be the reason. Assuming the three campaigns went out by email and your email tool kept open and click data. Assuming you can send plain emails to all 1,200 addresses without new tools.

**What's Likely Going On**
- They left for reasons a discount can't fix, such as moving, schedule changes, injury, or losing the habit (guess) — check: ask them; if most replies name a non-price reason, discounts were the wrong tool.
- The emails never reached them or were never opened — check: open rates on the three campaigns; very low opens mean the offers were never seen, not turned down.
- They saw the offer but the hard part is restarting, not cost (guess) — check: clicks versus sign-ups; many clicks with few joins point here, few clicks point to the first explanation.

**Recommendation**
- Do this first: Row 1 — pull the open and click data from the three campaigns, because if the emails weren't seen, every next email will fail the same way.
