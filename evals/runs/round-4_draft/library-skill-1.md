Assuming the drop is specific to after-school hours and the one pilot can be split into smaller parts, the first job is to find the cause before spending the budget.

**What's Likely Going On**
- Something the library changed since 2022 (hours, a cut program, staff, rules, or the teen space) pushed teens out — check: do teen visits by hour drop right after a dated change in the branch's schedule, program, or staffing log?
- Something outside the library changed (school bell times, bus routes, a new rec center or after-school program, more time on phones) — check: did other branches in the city lose teen visits at the same rate and time? If yes, the cause is likely outside this branch.
- Teens find the branch unwelcoming or not useful for what they do after school (a guess) — check: what do lapsed teens say they do instead, and where? If they name a place with group space, food, or fewer rules, this fits.
