# Example: Career Decision

## Input

Problem: Should I leave my stable job to join a startup?
Background: 8 years experience, two kids, six months of savings.

## Output

Summary: The choice depends on what you regret least in ten years, not on which job pays more now. Test startup life before you commit. Six months of savings is thin for two kids.

| Priority | Solution | Lens Tags | When To Use This Lens | Rationale | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Take a two-week unpaid trial with the startup | Design Thinking, PDCA | You can test before you commit | Real exposure beats hypotheticals | Startup may not allow it |
| 2 | Negotiate a part-time or contract role first | TRIZ, Optionality | You want both paths | Keeps the door open on both sides | Startup may see it as low commitment |
| 3 | Stay, but set a 6-month exit trigger | Regret Minimization, Cynefin | The decision is not urgent | Removes the pressure to decide today | You may drift past the trigger |
| 4 | Save to 12 months before deciding | First Principles, Occam's Razor | Savings are the real constraint | More runway makes the choice less risky | Delays the decision by months |
| 5 | Ask five people who made the jump | Six Thinking Hats, CPS | You lack real data | Secondhand data is cheap and fast | Survivorship bias skews the sample |
| Anchor | Write down your ten-year regret | Occam's Razor | You are stuck | One clear question cuts through the noise | You may rationalize the answer |

Ranked by: Coverage (default). Other options: Impact vs. Effort, Confidence vs. Reversibility, ICE, RICE, Eisenhower, Regret Minimization, Optionality, Cost of Delay.
