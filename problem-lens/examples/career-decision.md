# Example: Career Decision

## Input

Problem: Should I leave my stable job to join a startup?
Background: 8 years experience, two kids, six months of savings.

## Output

_Recorded run: Claude Opus 5.5, 2026-10-03, problem-lens 1.1.0. AskUserQuestion was not available, so the numbered-list prompt appears. Unedited._

**Summary**
"Should I leave?" may be the symptom; the likely root is either why you want out or whether your family can absorb the startup failing. Assuming the startup pays less cash than your current job and you have a specific offer in hand, six months of savings covers one job search but not a pay cut followed by a job search. Assuming no one else's income is certain, the downside lands on your kids too, so the money terms matter as much as the role.

**What's Likely Going On**
- The pull: this startup offers something your job can't (mission, growth, equity) — check: would any other new job feel as good, or only this one?
- The push: something in your current job is the real problem (stalled growth, a bad manager, boredom) — check: if your job fixed that one thing tomorrow, would you still go?
- The block is risk, not desire (guess) — check: does the offer's cash pay cover your monthly costs, and does the startup have at least 18 months of funding?

**Recommendation**
- Do this first: Row 1 — write down why you want to leave, because if the push comes from your current job, a safer move may fix it.
- Switch to Row 6 if your answer shows the push comes from your current job, not the pull of this startup.

**Questions That Would Change the Pick**
1. Does the offer's cash pay cover your monthly costs? If yes, your savings become a buffer, not your runway, and Row 4 matters less. If no, Row 3 moves to first.
2. Does someone else in your household earn a steady income? A second income makes leaving much safer; if you're the sole earner, Rows 5 and 6 move up.
3. How many months of funding does the startup have? Under 12 months points toward waiting or Row 6; 18 or more makes the leap more reasonable.
