**Summary.** "Leave or stay" is the choice you see, but the likely root is two things you haven't said yet: what your current job is missing, and whether your family can absorb the startup failing. Assuming you have a real offer in hand, six months of savings covers your full family costs, and your household relies mostly on your income. With two kids, the downside matters more than the upside, so the real question is whether you can make the downside survivable.

**Recommendation.**
- Do this first: Row 1 — write down why you want to leave and test whether that need can be met without quitting, because if it can, you skip the risk entirely.
- Switch to Row 2 if the reason is something only a startup can give you, such as building from zero or a mission you care about.
