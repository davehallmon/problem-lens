**Summary.** The bankruptcy started this, but the root is that one supplier carried all your stock, so losing it leaves you about 1,200 orders from empty. Assuming the 4 days is an average across products, some items will sell out sooner, and neither backup has been tested on quality or speed. Assuming you can pay up front if a new supplier asks you to.

**Recommendation.**
- `Do this first: Row 1 — order from both backup suppliers today, because their lead time is the one thing you cannot shorten later.`
- `Switch to Row 2 if both backups quote lead times longer than 4 days.`
