**Summary.** Three failed discounts are a symptom: the likely root is that you don't yet know why these 1,200 people left, so you keep testing price, which may not be the reason. Assuming you can see open and click rates for the past campaigns and have basic records (join date, last visit, cancel date), the first job is to find the real reason before you spend on a fourth offer. Losing 1,200 members in 18 months may also mean a retention leak in your current members.

**What's Likely Going On**
- The emails never reached people (spam folders, dead addresses, ignored sender) — check: open and click rates for the three campaigns; very low opens point here, normal opens rule it out.
- Price was not why they left; they lost the habit, felt awkward coming back, or had a bad experience — check: whether people opened and clicked but did not sign up, plus what they say when asked directly. (Guess: this is the most common pattern for gyms.)
- They are gone for good: moved, joined another gym, or quit exercise — check: whether lapses cluster after a specific event (new competitor, price change, schedule cut) and what share of replies say "moved" or "joined elsewhere."

**Recommendation**
- Do this first: Row 1 — pull open and click rates for all three campaigns, because if nobody saw the offers, you don't know yet whether discounts work.
- Switch to Row 6 if lapsed members far outnumber your active members — then stopping the leak matters more than winning people back.
