# problem-lens

![problem-lens banner: Twelve lenses. Six moves. One first step.](assets/banner.png)

A Claude Skill that turns a messy problem into ranked options and a clear place to start.

## Get It

[github.com/davehallmon/problem-lens](https://github.com/davehallmon/problem-lens)

## What It Does

- Twelve lenses. Six moves. One first step.
- Five solutions from five lens families, plus an Occam's Razor anchor.
- Nine ranking frameworks.
- A decision prompt at the end.
- Bias guards built in.

## How It Was Tested

Version 1.1.0 went 5–2–4 against plain Claude on eleven problems, scored by blind judges. Full results, including where it trails, are in [`evals/`](https://github.com/davehallmon/problem-lens/tree/main/evals).

## The Family

problem-lens covers *what to do*. Its sibling, [reasoning-lens](https://github.com/davehallmon/reasoning-lens), covers *how to think*: seven philosophers' methods, one idea, and where they disagree.
